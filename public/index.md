## Why agents get stuck

- Prices load with JavaScript: the agent sees an empty price.
- Icon-only buttons: the agent can't tell what they do.
- Unlabeled form fields: the agent can't fill them in.
- No machine-readable summary: the agent doesn't know what you sell.
- No sitemap, llms.txt or AI rules: the agent can't find its way.

No error. No alert. Just a sale you never knew you lost.

## The Agent Readiness Score

12 checks in three groups:

- **Find**: Can AI agents find your site and know what it offers?
- **Read**: Can agents understand your content: prices, products, details?
- **Act**: Can agents complete tasks like booking, buying or signing up?

| Range | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0–40 | Not ready | Most AI agents can't use your site. They skip you and pick a competitor. |
| 41–70 | Partly ready | Agents find you, then get stuck on prices, forms or buttons. |
| 71–100 | Agent-ready | Agents can read your site and finish tasks. |
